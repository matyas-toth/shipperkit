# Support

- **Questions and help:** ask on [Discord](https://discord.shipperkit.dev).
- **Guides:** the [documentation](https://shipperkit.dev/docs/) covers installation, deployments, domains, backups and recovery.
- **Bugs and feature requests:** open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/matyas-toth/shipperkit/issues/new/choose).
- **Security issues:** follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) and never post them publicly.
